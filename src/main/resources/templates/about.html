About Us

Pathfinder is a platform for sharing and discovering routes across Bulgaria.

🏔

Real Routes

Every route is added by a real user with a GPX track, interactive map and description.

🗺

Interactive Maps

Every route is visualised on OpenStreetMap with the full GPX track, distance and start point.

📷

Photos & Video

Authors upload photos and attach YouTube videos to give a full picture before you set off.

💬

Community

Leave comments, share impressions and help fellow travellers choose the right route.
